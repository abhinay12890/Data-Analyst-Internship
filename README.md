# Data Analyst Internship Projects
This repository contains a curated collection of data analytics projects completed during my Data Analyst Internship (recreated using non-confidential/sample datasets).  
It includes Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) notebooks and Tableau dashboards across finance, e-commerce, entertainment, travel, manufacturing, logistics, and public-safety domains.

Each project demonstrates:

- Data Cleaning & Preprocessing  
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
- Visualization & Insight Generation   
- Tableau Dashboards for business reporting  
- Python-based analytics workflows (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)

## Projects

### **1. TATA Steel EDA**
Business Problem

Analyze manufacturing data to identify factors contributing to machine failures and improve operational reliability through predictive maintenance.

Business Recommendations

Implement a predictive maintenance system using key operational parameters (tool wear, rotational speed, torque, air temperature, and process temperature) to detect potential failures early, reducing unplanned downtime and maintenance costs.
Optimize machine operating conditions by improving temperature control, maintaining safe rotational speed and torque ranges, and proactively monitoring tool wear to minimize heat dissipation and overstrain failures while improving product quality.
Establish real-time monitoring and alerting for critical machine parameters to enable proactive maintenance scheduling, improve operational efficiency, and reduce unexpected equipment failures.

### **2. Netflix EDA**
Business Problem

Analyze Netflix's content library to understand content distribution, release trends, and regional diversity for improving content strategy.

Business Recommendations

Diversify the content library by expanding TV Shows, underrepresented genres, and region-specific content to improve audience reach, engagement, and content diversity.
Strengthen content acquisition and production strategies by investing in highly rated, trending, and multi-season content while maintaining a balanced mix of movies and TV shows to maximize long-term viewer retention.
Enhance personalized content discovery by improving the recommendation engine using genre preferences, viewing trends, and regional content patterns to increase content visibility and customer satisfaction. 

### **3. Prime Video EDA**

Business Problem

Analyze Prime Video's content library to understand content distribution, release trends, and regional diversity for improving content strategy.
Business Recommendations
Diversify the content portfolio by expanding genre variety and investing in genre-blended content to broaden audience appeal, improve user engagement, and strengthen subscriber retention.
Prioritize acquisition and promotion of high-quality content by investing in titles with strong IMDb/TMDb ratings and high audience engagement while increasing the visibility of highly rated but less popular content.
Enhance the recommendation engine by incorporating genre preferences, regional viewing trends, and user ratings to deliver more personalized content recommendations and support long-term subscription growth.

### **4. Yes Bank EDA**
Business Problem

Analyze historical stock price trends to identify market patterns that support informed investment decisions.

Business Recommendations

Adopt trend-following and momentum-based trading strategies by leveraging recurring monthly price patterns and directional price movements to identify potential trading opportunities.
Incorporate volatility and risk monitoring into investment decisions by capitalizing on stable trading periods while continuously tracking market conditions to adapt strategies and manage risk exposure.
Develop predictive models using historical price relationships to forecast future price movements, improve trading decisions, and support data-driven investment strategies.

### **5. Flipkart EDA**
Business Problem

Analyze customer support data to identify service bottlenecks and improve operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

Business Recommendations

Optimize customer support operations by reducing email response times through automated ticket routing and increasing staffing during peak demand periods to improve service efficiency.
Address product-specific support issues by improving product quality for Electronics, streamlining refund and return processes for Lifestyle products, and resolving payment-related issues for Mobiles to reduce recurring complaints.
Improve support performance through targeted agent training, recognition of top performers, and city-level resource planning to enhance customer experience.

### **6. Airbnb Data Cleaning**
- Performed cleaning of Airbnb listing data

### **7. FedEx Logistics EDA**
Business Problem

Analyze logistics operations to reduce shipping costs, improve delivery performance, and optimize supply chain efficiency.

Business Recommendations

Optimize shipment planning by selecting the most cost-effective transportation modes and delivery terms based on country, product category, and urgency.
Strengthen logistics performance by monitoring vendor reliability and addressing recurring delays in high-risk regions while leveraging consistently high-performing vendors.
Improve supply chain efficiency through data-driven inventory and logistics planning using delivery lead times, freight costs, shipment weights, and product risk profiles.
### **8. Ford Bike Sharing EDA**
Business Problem

Analyze bike-sharing usage patterns to improve fleet utilization, customer experience, and operational planning.

Business Recommendations

Optimize fleet allocation and maintenance schedules by prioritizing bike availability at high-demand stations and during peak commuting hours.
Increase customer acquisition and subscription conversion through targeted promotions for casual riders, younger users, and underrepresented demographics while introducing flexible pricing plans for longer leisure rides.
Enhance operational efficiency using trip demand patterns to align staffing, bike redistribution, and station capacity with peak usage periods and rider behavior.

### **9 & 10. Tableau Dashboards**
**Airbnb Dashboard:**
Business Problem

Analyze Airbnb listings to identify pricing, occupancy, and host performance factors that improve booking performance.

Business Recommendations

Implement dynamic pricing strategies by aligning listing prices with neighborhood demand, property type, room type, and review performance.
Encourage hosts to achieve Superhost status and complete identity verification to strengthen guest trust and increase booking potential.
Promote high-performing neighborhoods and property types while expanding affordable listing options to attract diverse traveler segments. 

**FBI Crime Dashboard:**
Business Problem

Analyze historical crime data to identify crime hotspots, temporal trends, and resource allocation opportunities for public safety.

Business Recommendations
Increase law enforcement presence and surveillance in high-crime neighborhoods and street blocks, particularly around theft hotspots.
Deploy additional patrols during peak crime periods using temporal crime patterns to optimize police resource allocation.
Adopt hotspot-driven policing and predictive monitoring using geospatial analysis and historical crime trends to proactively reduce repeat offenses.
